# Chapter 1: The Fractured Mirror

Clayton's routine cracks the moment he catches a ghost in the signal. What looks like a stray reflection in the office window becomes a second version of himself, standing still, watching, and then vanishing as the fluorescent lights flicker.

## Seeking Answers

A diagnostic at Nexum returns nothing, yet the SCN whispers in the background of every device. Clayton begins to log the glitches, tracing them to a frequency he cannot hear but can feel.

## The First Ripple

That night, the mirror in his apartment splinters without a sound. In its shards, the city appears fractured, the SCN threading through alleys and towers like a lattice of light.
