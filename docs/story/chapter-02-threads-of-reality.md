Clayton follows the anomalies through New Elysium's transit grid, where the SCN bleeds through the signal relays. A pattern emerges: every glitch coincides with a surge in the city's hidden network.

## Seamus Appears

An older man steps into Clayton's path, introducing himself as Seamus O'Driscoll. He speaks of the SCN as if it were a living river, warning Clayton not to drink from it.

## A Dangerous Invitation

Seamus offers a choice: continue ignoring the fractures or learn to navigate them. Clayton, desperate for meaning, follows him into a dimly lit service corridor humming with unseen data.
