# Chapter 6: Echoes in the Void

At the abyssal core, the SCN speaks with a voice that is not a voice. Clayton feels it mapping his thoughts, rewriting them in real time.

## The Void's Lesson

In the void, he meets echoes of other travelers who lost their names. Their warnings are fragmented, but their fear is unmistakable.

## A Fragile Thread

Clayton grasps a fragile thread of control, enough to pull himself back. He understands now that the SCN will never release him without a cost.
