# Chapter 7: Shiniza Yakavaze's Paradox

Clayton emerges to find New Elysium warped by a new presence. Shiniza Yakavaze, bound to a wheelchair but unbound in the SCN, bends reality as if it were a script.

## The Paradox Revealed

Shiniza speaks of control and liberation in the same breath, claiming the SCN is both a prison and a key. His body, ruined by the network, is a warning wrapped in power.

## The Unsteady Alliance

Clayton must decide whether to oppose Shiniza or learn from him. The paradox is clear: to resist the SCN's domination, Clayton may have to wield it himself.
