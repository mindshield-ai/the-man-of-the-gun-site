# Chapter 4: The Weight of Conviction

Seamus takes Clayton to the edge of the network, to a temple of humming machines that once belonged to the Dining Philosophers. The air is heavy with conviction, like a vow etched into the circuitry.

## The Philosophers' Creed

Records reveal the Philosophers' belief: humanity must be guided by those who can interpret the SCN. Their creed demands sacrifice, and their followers enforce it with ruthless certainty.

## A Choice to Carry

Clayton realizes the SCN is no longer just a mystery but a burden. He must decide whether to bear its weight or let it crush him.
