The table stood alone in the dim backroom, its surface scarred by years of quiet deals and unspoken vows. Clayton rested his hands on the worn wood, the lingering hum of the SCN still thrumming in his bones. Beyond the cracked window, dawn crept over New Elysium, painting the city in bruised light.

He wasn’t sure if he had saved anything—only that he had chosen what came next. The monolith’s shadow had lifted, but the weight of conviction remained. Across the table, an empty chair waited, an invitation and a warning all at once. Clayton exhaled, let the silence settle, and sat.
